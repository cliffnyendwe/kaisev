Take Control of Your Freight

The Real Cost of Letting Your Supplier Handle Freight

When you buy on CIF (Cost, Insurance, Freight) terms, your overseas supplier arranges the ocean or air freight and insurance on your behalf, then bundles those costs into your invoice.


When you buy on FOB (Free On Board) terms, the supplier's responsibility ends once the goods are loaded onto the vessel or aircraft at the port or airport of origin. From that point, you — or your appointed freight forwarder — control the freight, insurance, routing and customs process all the way to your warehouse in Kenya.


That's where Kai-Sev comes in.
